Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This messaging service sends a personal, automated daily text message containing S&P 500 stock market index updates. It is operated by a single individual for personal, non-commercial use.

Data Collection

The only phone number used by this service is the number belonging to the account owner, who is also the sole recipient of messages. No phone numbers belonging to any other individuals are collected, stored, or messaged.

Data Sharing

No mobile phone number or personal information collected through this service is shared, sold, or disclosed to any third party, affiliate, or lead generator at any time, for any purpose.

Opt-Out

The recipient may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for support.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the account owner directly.
